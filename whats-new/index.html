Ellis 1.6.5 · November 19, 2026

Your business's own numbers in SEO (Pro)

Connect the software your business runs and its numbers show up beside the SEO numbers: leads, reservations, move-ins, occupancy and revenue, on the site's page and in the client report. Hummingbird (self-storage) is the first one built in. Ellis only reads.

Customer journey on the Scorecard

One chart that follows a site from a Google search to a sale: found, visited, asked, reserved, rented. For any dates, next to the time before, with every number its source's own.

Invoices from another system

Bill some customers through Bill.com, FreshBooks, Square or anything else? Import the file and those invoices count in the Scorecard and show on each company's timeline. Import a newer file any time: nothing is doubled, and an import can be undone.

Faster answers, and long ones finish

Replies on Mistral are quick again. A long draft or summary now arrives instead of timing out, and the chat stays connected while Ellis looks something up.

A heads-up before your updates end

The daily brief tells the owner 30 days ahead. If your updates have ended, a newer installer stops by itself and the Ellis you have keeps working.

Working hours, and a guide you can trust

Set your working hours for each day in Settings, so Find a time looks inside them. The user guide was checked line by line against the app and corrected.

Ellis 1.6.0 · November 12, 2026A 90-day clean-up, leaner storage, more leads for fewer credits and a password reset.

Clean-up

One list of what has gone quiet for 90 days: leads, deals that aren't moving, untouched tasks, quotes with no answer and drafts never sent. Keep what matters, remove the rest one at a time or a whole group.

Nothing is erased straight away

What you remove goes to a recycle bin and can be put back as it was for 30 days. Anything that went out to a client, and tasks with time logged, are never removed.

Leaner storage

Photos are shrunk as you add them, still sharp enough to read, and once a day Ellis tidies its own old files, so it stays small and quick.

More leads for fewer credits (Pro, with a Hunter account)

More owners are found, a company you already paid to check is skipped for free for 90 days, and replies from every sequence in your Hunter account are followed.

An assistant that stays on task

Ellis only proposes changes about the clients and people you mentioned. Long replies end cleanly, and long chats send less with every message, which lowers the AI cost.

Forgot your password?

Reset the owner's password from the Ellis icon by the clock (on a Mac, in the menu bar). It only works on the computer Ellis runs on.

Ellis 1.5.3 · November 5, 2026Sales dashboards, QuickBooks and Stripe sync, assets, and lead finding anywhere.

A Scorecard like HubSpot or Pipedrive

Pipeline by stage, a forecast of what's likely to close, win rate and why deals were lost, where your leads come from, each person's activity, profit by month and your top clients. Pick any dates, click a bar to see the deals behind it, and download it all as a PDF.

QuickBooks and Stripe, in step every night

Your customers, products, invoices, payments, expenses and Stripe fees come into Ellis, so your numbers are complete even for work billed outside Ellis. A new customer in Ellis is added to QuickBooks and Stripe on its own: no typing anything in twice.

Assets and depreciation

Mark a big purchase (a computer, a truck, equipment) as an asset and Ellis writes it off month by month. See what you own and what it's worth now, mark it sold, and export the list for your accountant.

This quarter and last quarter

One click on Expenses and Mileage, for quarterly taxes and reviews.

Find leads anywhere (Pro, with a Hunter account)

Any US state, your own areas, or describe the businesses you want in your own words. Leave out what kind or where, and Ellis asks first.

A cleaner sales pipeline

Companies move from Lead to Contacted to Customer, and "Not a fit" keeps the ones that won't buy out of your way. Deals have an owner, and a reason when they're lost.

Break a big job into pieces, each with its own person and date, and mark what can't start until something else is done.

Check off your day

Find leads in bulk (Pro, with a Hunter account)
